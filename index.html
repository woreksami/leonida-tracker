      How the estimate works

      First boot

      The Leonida developers expect booting to take 1 to 3 days after a retail exe reaches them. The tracker starts from that window and stretches or shrinks it by how fast work is landing now: the pull requests merged in the last 24 hours against the 24 hours before, capped at ±25% so one big merge round can't swing it wildly.

      waiting for data…

      When the coordination board moves the boot task (RT-5) from "blocked" to claimed, the sign says work is underway. When a pull request for RT-5 merges, it says booted.

      Graphics layer

      Items on the graphics board that aren't merged yet, divided by how many graphics-layer pull requests merged per hour over the last two days. The range uses the last 24 hours as the second speed.

      waiting for data…

      Playable has no estimate on purpose: nobody can know how much breaks until the game boots.

      Data: the public Leonida repository on GitHub, read from your browser. Estimates are projections, not promises.

      Fan-made and unofficial. Not affiliated with Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive or Microsoft. Leonida ships no game files: players supply their own legally owned copy. The exe mentioned above stays on the tester's PC and is never shared.
